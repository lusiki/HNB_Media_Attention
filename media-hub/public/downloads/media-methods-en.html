Media overview

Methods and limitations

What is included?

Web records whose source domain exactly matches the current Agency for Electronic Media register are eligible. A domain is used as the outlet identity. Official HNB and identified institutional or association channels are separated; social media and audience discussion are outside the main series. Current registration does not prove historical registration or Croatian-language content. Ownership and complete outlet capture remain unknown.

What is an HNB mention?

The retained title or body must contain the bounded acronym HNB (including Croatian case suffixes) or a form of Hrvatska narodna banka / Croatian National Bank. Generic central-bank phrases, officeholder surnames and snippet-only matches are separate candidate diagnostics. Mentions may be incidental, historical or critical. They are not assertions that an action attributed to HNB really occurred.

How are publications counted?

Repeated captures on the same outlet and normalized article URL are merged. If an article URL is unavailable, the fallback uses source, record date and normalized title. The longest captured body is retained and mentions are checked again. First record date sets the month. URL updates can be merged; URL variants can survive. Cross-outlet copies count separately because outlet publication is the unit. Exact text hashes are a limited diagnostic, not a count of unique stories.

What does the rate divide by?

The rate is 10,000 times HNB-mention publications passing the archive’s standalone-i query divided by all publications passing that same query in the same eligible domains and months. Both sides use the same publication identity. From January 2024 the API batch was collected with this filter; the legacy batch is filtered locally to match. The unknown original legacy selection and changing vendor capture still prevent a national market-share interpretation.

Where are the boundaries?

The current archive contains records for every calendar day from January 2021 to 10 September 2026. This establishes date coverage, not complete article capture. Its collection regime changes in January 2024. The repaired first quarter of 2024 is available here, while the frozen inflation study retains its missing media period and April 2024 boundary. September is partial and excluded from headline totals.

What was reviewed?

A deterministic development sample covered retrieval routes and both collection regimes, supplemented by source-stratified explicit hits, exact-text groups and 32 background non-hits. The audit removed a currency-converter footer and identified related-content links. Remaining page navigation can still create matches. This was an agent-led development audit. There is no independent human validation, precision/recall estimate or validated subject classification.
