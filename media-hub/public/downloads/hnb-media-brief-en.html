Media overview

The Croatian National Bank in Croatian media

January 2021–August 2026 · September available through 10 September · research release candidate, author review pending.

V1 · 33,235 publications mention HNB explicitly

The captured sample includes 281 registered outlets. Each source–publication identity counts once; repeated captures are merged. This measures observed publication presence, not audience exposure.

V2 · Five outlets account for 23.9% of the sample

The largest contributors are ranked by publication count within the same full-month window. This is concentration across outlet domains; ownership and readership are not measured.

V3 · 15.7% also name HNB in the title

5,222 retained titles contain the acronym or formal name. A title match is a narrow observable marker. It does not establish the bank’s main role, quotation, stance or substantive prominence.

Coverage extends across several institutional subjects

The development audit found coverage of loans, cash and euro conversion, bank supervision, statistics and governance. Eight overlapping keyword screens support exploration; they are not validated topic-prevalence estimates.

Count and denominator

The rate is 10,000 times HNB-mention publications passing the archive’s standalone-i query divided by all publications passing that same query in the same eligible domains and months. Both sides use the same publication identity. From January 2024 the API batch was collected with this filter; the legacy batch is filtered locally to match. The unknown original legacy selection and changing vendor capture still prevent a national market-share interpretation.

Validation and limits

A deterministic development sample covered retrieval routes and both collection regimes, supplemented by source-stratified explicit hits, exact-text groups and 32 background non-hits. The audit removed a currency-converter footer and identified related-content links. Remaining page navigation can still create matches. This was an agent-led development audit. There is no independent human validation, precision/recall estimate or validated subject classification.

The linked inflation study

The extended inflation study by Petra Palić and Luka Sikić (17 September 2026; author review pending) examines weighted institutional visibility inside an inflation corpus through May 2026. Its primary weekly association is a 0.21 percentage-point lower weighted share per one percentage-point higher inflation; time controls qualify that association. A stable link to consumer expectations is not established. Those models and their saved inputs have not been rerun for this release.

Added in this edition

New source rates require at least 5,000 eligible background publications and 50 HNB publications (78 domains qualify). The effective number of domains is 38.44. Monthly title shares, source breadth and equal-weight comparisons now accompany counts; these remain descriptive archive measures.
