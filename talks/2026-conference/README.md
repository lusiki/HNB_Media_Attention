# Reading the economy through the News

Talk by Luka Sikić, Hrvatsko katoličko sveučilište, at the 30th Young Statisticians Meeting,
Mönichwald, Austria, Sunday 4 October 2026 (Session 6, 11:00 to 11:30).
Based on the working paper by Petra Palić and Luka Sikić, version of 28 September 2026:
https://lusiki.github.io/HNB_Media_Attention/media/downloads/PAPER_EXT.html

- `HNB-talk-slides.html`: the presentation, self contained, opens in any browser and works offline
- `HNB-talk-slides.pdf`: the same 19 slides, one per page

The slides follow the meeting's structure: outline and keywords, introduction, literature review,
data and methods, results and discussion, conclusions with limitations and key takeaways, bibliography.
