# Publication evidence register

Presentation version: 2026-09-19.2. The bilingual page and two-page briefs interpret the 17 September 2026 extended manuscript by Petra Palić and Luka Sikić. Author review is pending; the website does not re-estimate the econometric models.

The current numerical provenance, selected specifications, descriptive aggregations and external media-context sources are documented in [inspection-evidence.md](inspection-evidence.md). Fixed finding records are in [findings.json](../public/data/findings.json); canonical research values are in [evidence.json](../public/data/evidence.json).

The first September 19 revision added the evidence overview, measurement guide, worked examples, practical investigation agenda and precise interval interpretation. The second added question lenses, contextual inspection, direct comparisons, concentration summaries and traceable records. The two-page briefs use the same qualified conclusions as the website.

`verify.py` and `verify_inspection.py` validate the publication contracts. Their optional `--research-root` checks compare with saved research outputs and hashes. Browser suites check the actual interactions, downloads, keyboard behavior, text enlargement and brief readers. Private provenance, previous snapshots and the original manuscript remain outside the public bundle.
