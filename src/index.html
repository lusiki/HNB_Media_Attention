Network position is a different measure

A share of coverage describes relative representation. Network centrality describes an institution’s position among sources and actors. The common-source network check does not establish lasting structural erosion; the two measures should not be substituted for one another.

06 / Methods & limits

A measure of relative visibility.

Return to the figure ↑

Reading the sign

Attention gap =
baseline share − current share

A positive gap means lower visibility than the baseline. A negative gap means higher visibility. Zero marks that reference, not an ideal level of communication.

The displayed monthly baseline is @@BASELINE@@%: the mean weighted share in January–June 2021 after excluding baseline observations more than two standard deviations from the initial mean. Monthly and weekly baselines are calculated separately.

What enters the measure?

The numerator adds potential reach weighted by institutional salience for inflation-relevant items in the HNB corpus. The denominator adds reach across the inflation corpus, plus one. Salience combines title presence, first-mention position, mention frequency and institutional agency cues.

Formal names, acronyms and generic central-bank terms identify the institutional corpus. Generic terms can include other central banks, including ECB or Eurosystem coverage after euro adoption. The measure does not cleanly isolate every HNB message.

Sample, platforms and coverage

The primary sample has 62 eligible months and 270 eligible weeks through May 2026; the main regressions use 60 months and 268 weeks after within-segment changes. January–March 2024 is excluded monthly; the complete week beginning 1 January 2024 remains eligible weekly.

The analysis preserves the earlier platform universe. Newly added TV, radio, print, Bluesky and Threads are excluded. The April 2024 export uses a broader query and a standalone “i” filter. This is a selected vendor corpus, not a complete census of Croatian media or a validated Croatian audience sample.

Collection interruptions, platform entry and changes in selection can alter composition. Source labels alone do not establish stable coverage. Recorded dates are used as the time basis; their interpretation as publication timestamps is not established here.

Later observed records run to 10 September 2026 according to the dataset documentation. June onward has a vendor sentiment change; August–September has incomplete coverage. None extends the headline sample beyond May 2026.

Model and uncertainty

The baseline models control for the change in inflation, euro adoption, concentration of source reach, vendor negative-sentiment share and the source change. Equation 9b also includes log HNB-corpus volume. Constant controls are omitted within a single regime.

Reported 95% intervals use the saved segment-aware Newey–West estimates with Bartlett weights, bandwidth floor(N1/3) and no prewhitening. Changes, lags and residual products do not cross the text-coverage gap. Weekly macroeconomic values are assigned from the month of the week’s start.

All public estimates were matched to saved outputs. This website build did not re-estimate the research models.

What the study cannot establish

Potential reach is not observed reading, exposure, trust or understanding. Some reach values are imputed. Vendor sentiment is not independently validated on this corpus. The gap is neither a count of HNB statements nor a score of communication quality.

HANFA comparisons and instrument diagnostics do not establish an HNB-specific causal mechanism. Forward expectation results depend on interpolation and controls. Media evidence alone cannot establish anchoring of expectations or changes in individual beliefs.

The older paper’s abstract, table and recalculated initial-sample coefficients do not all agree. The extended draft reports this unresolved discrepancy; it does not claim exact legacy replication.
