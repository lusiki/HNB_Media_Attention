01 / Understand the measure

A share of the debate.
A starting point for investigation.

The study weights institutional items by their estimated potential reach and how prominently the institution appears. It compares that total with potential reach across monitored inflation coverage. This is a constructed weighted share, not the proportion of people paying attention or a simple percentage of articles.

Relative visibility can fall even when institutional coverage stays unchanged, if the wider inflation discussion grows faster. That is a possible mechanism to investigate, not a decomposition established by the headline model.

04 / Proposed applied research

How are monetary-policy explanations
represented in Croatian media?

A focused pilot would follow a small, agreed set of explanations through a defined period of coverage. It would test whether the evidence is reliable and useful enough for recurring analysis.

01

Validate the measurement

Review collection continuity and manually validate HNB, ECB/Eurosystem, other central-bank and ambiguous references.

Deliverable: attribution and coverage audit, with reliability estimates and documented limitations.

02

Trace selected explanations

Compare source communications with media coverage: which explanations appear, which channels carry them and whether their meaning is preserved.

Deliverable: message-uptake case studies and a validated analytical dataset.

03

Assess the next decision

Identify topics or channels for closer review and assess whether a recurring analytical brief would add useful information.

Deliverable: an executive briefing and a recommendation on further monitoring or audience research.

Why the expectations result depends on survey timing

The survey is monthly. Assigning values to weeks in different ways does not create new weekly responses. A linear assignment moves gradually between monthly values; a step assignment holds a monthly value across its weeks. Results also change with the control set.

Network position answers a different question

A share of coverage describes relative representation. Network centrality describes an institution’s position among sources and actors. The common-source network check does not establish lasting structural erosion; it should not be substituted for the visibility measure.

07 / Methods & limits

What the indicator can carry.

Return to the figure ↑

Reading the sign

Attention gap =
baseline share − current share

A positive gap means lower visibility than the baseline. A negative gap means higher visibility. Zero marks that reference, not an ideal level of communication.

The displayed monthly baseline is @@BASELINE@@%: the mean weighted share in January–June 2021 after excluding baseline observations more than two standard deviations from the initial mean. Monthly and weekly baselines are calculated separately.

What enters the measure?

The numerator adds potential reach weighted by institutional salience for inflation-relevant items in the HNB corpus. The denominator adds reach across the inflation corpus, plus one. Salience combines title presence, first-mention position, mention frequency and institutional agency cues.

Formal names, acronyms and generic central-bank terms identify the institutional corpus. Generic terms can include other central banks, including ECB or Eurosystem coverage after euro adoption. The measure does not cleanly isolate every HNB message. An HNB-focused pilot would validate HNB-specific, ECB/Eurosystem, other and ambiguous references, including joint attribution. That validation has not yet been completed.

Sample, platforms and coverage

The primary sample has 62 eligible months and 270 eligible weeks through May 2026; the main regressions use 60 months and 268 weeks after within-segment changes. January–March 2024 is excluded monthly; the complete week beginning 1 January 2024 remains eligible weekly.

The analysis preserves the earlier platform universe. Newly added TV, radio, print, Bluesky and Threads are excluded. The April 2024 export uses a broader query and a standalone “i” filter. This is a selected vendor corpus, not a complete census of Croatian media or a validated Croatian audience sample.

Collection interruptions, platform entry and changes in selection can alter composition. Source labels alone do not establish stable coverage. Recorded dates are used as the time basis; their interpretation as publication timestamps is not established here.

Later observed records run to 10 September 2026 according to the dataset documentation. June onward has a vendor sentiment change; August–September has incomplete coverage. None extends the headline sample beyond May 2026.

Model and uncertainty

The baseline models control for the change in inflation, euro adoption, concentration of source reach, vendor negative-sentiment share and the source change. Equation 9b also includes log HNB-corpus volume. Constant controls are omitted within a single regime.

Reported 95% intervals use the saved segment-aware Newey–West estimates with Bartlett weights, bandwidth floor(N1/3) and no prewhitening. Changes, lags and residual products do not cross the text-coverage gap. Weekly macroeconomic values are assigned from the month of the week’s start.

All public estimates were matched to saved outputs. This website build did not re-estimate the research models.

What the study cannot establish

Potential reach is not observed reading, exposure, trust or understanding. Some reach values are imputed. Vendor sentiment is not independently validated on this corpus. The gap is neither a count of HNB statements nor a score of communication quality. No threshold for policy-relevant deterioration or monetary welfare cost is established.

HANFA comparisons and instrument diagnostics do not establish an HNB-specific causal mechanism. Forward expectation results depend on interpolation and controls. Media evidence alone cannot establish anchoring of expectations or changes in individual beliefs.

The older paper’s abstract, table and recalculated initial-sample coefficients do not all agree. The extended draft reports this unresolved discrepancy; it does not claim exact legacy replication.
